# Design System: Study Hub — Study Studio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Contemporary University Library"**

A bright student library turns six course identities into coloured book covers, clear shelves of resources and a persistent route home. Cool paper, cobalt invitations and locally bundled Alexandria make the Arabic interface feel welcoming while English academic titles share its display voice.

The human explicitly requested plentiful exuberant animation. Springing cards, a persistent Canvas constellation, floating books and a branching source atlas make discovery continuously interactive; the reader stays quiet. Motion starts automatically, with system reduced motion providing a static fallback. On an eligible first desktop session, Study Rift opens with an iridescent procedural sculpture, a scroll-directed tunnel flight and arrival at the Study Hub title before the Studio home; permanent Canvas and Atlas interactions remain available after it. Native links, selectors and scrolling retain their ordinary behavior.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Course colour continuity across books, cards, navigation and resource rows.
- A right rail on desktop and a bottom route bar on phones.
- Abundant automatic interaction around stable learning controls, with a static system reduced-motion fallback.
- Real library metadata and saved study state determine the interface.

## Colors

Cobalt supplies the invitation and selected state; soft subject washes identify academic shelves against cool paper. Frontmatter values are normative and come from the final stylesheet cascade.

### Primary
- **Library cobalt** (`accent`, `hero`): controls, active routes and the approved home study invitation. The original blue portal mark remains a binding identity asset.
- **Atlas cobalt** (`atlas-ground`, `atlas-branch`, `atlas-select`): the source-map field, branch buttons and native course selector; pale selected branches use `atlas-selected` and `atlas-action-ink`. Reading mode uses the corresponding `atlas-reading-*` forest roles. This is an explicit expansion of the library world, not drift from its previous record.

### Secondary
- **Study Rift spectral accents** (`rift-indigo`, `rift-aqua`, `rift-coral`): iridescent sculpture, tunnel rings and Canvas fallback. `rift-pearl` carries its wordmark against `rift-midnight`; supporting `rift-*` colours maintain readable copy, progress, focus and skip states. These scene-scoped roles extend the system without changing the approved Studio/Atlas palettes.
- **Book mint, book pink and book peach**: the hero links for Database Systems, Operating Systems and Data Structures respectively. These illustrations keep their pastel covers in all themes.
- **Course blue, purple, mint, peach, pink and sky**: six course washes paired with the corresponding `tone-*` ink; dark-theme subject icons instead use the theme's main ink for legibility.

### Neutral
- **Cool ground, white paper and indigo ink** (`bg`, `paper`, `ink`): page, surfaces and main text. `muted` supports metadata and `line` separates rows.
- **Night library** (`dark-*`): dark navy surfaces, pale main ink and a light periwinkle accent.
- **Reading garden** (`reading-*`): warm green paper, forest ink and a moss accent. Values absent from its override inherit the default root tokens, including focus, positive and negative colours; its warning is overridden again in the motion sheet.

**The Same Shelf Rule.** A course retains its colour family wherever it appears; theme changes alter its wash without changing its identity.

## Typography

**Display Font:** locally bundled Alexandria, with Tahoma and sans-serif fallbacks.
**Body Font:** Alexandria; the inherited body is 15px/1.8, while the operating interface deliberately uses compact 11–12px labels and metadata.
**Label/Mono Font:** utility English and course codes can retain Segoe UI/Arial; code uses Consolas/Courier New. These utilities are not the display pairing.

The local variable font includes Arabic and Latin subsets at weights 100–900 with swap loading. Display overrides in the final motion sheet win over earlier Segoe UI title rules. The frontmatter records roles; sizes vary with the documented component and viewport.

- **Display:** the hero title uses the display role; above 1600px its earlier high-specificity responsive declaration reaches 40px. At intermediate widths its explicit overrides use 29px, then the final phone rule uses 28px.
- **Headline:** view headings use the headline role; the greeting is 28px desktop and 22px phone. Atlas subject titles use 29px/1.55 at weight 600, 26px at 1300px, 21px/1.5 at 540px, and 19px below 360px.
- **Title:** course cards use 20px above 760px and 18px at 760px and below, where an inherited journey-sheet `!important` rule wins over the later 17px declarations; their winning font weight is 650; library titles use 16px and 15px phone; book titles use 15px.
- **Atlas source hierarchy:** collection titles use 22px/1.6, 20px at 800px, and 19px/1.5 at 540px; numbered-source titles use 18px/1.7 at weight 500, falling to 16px on phones. Branch labels are 12px desktop and 11px phone; real counts are 10px.
- **Rift opening:** local Alexandria sets the scene title at `clamp(34px, 4.7vw, 74px)` and its final Study Hub arrival at `clamp(55px, 9vw, 140px)`, both weight 650. Copy is 14px/1.9, reduced to 13px at 1200px. The wordmark is 21px/1.5; skip and footer controls are 12px/1.7. The scene has its own expressive scale without changing the learning interface.
- **Body / label:** compact metadata uses 11–12px with generous inherited leading. Keep Arabic sentences short and English metadata isolated with native bidi markup.

**The Shared Display Voice Rule.** Arabic and English display titles use the bundled Alexandria face; system fonts remain utility fallbacks, not the display direction.

The timetable shell and entries are deliberately unboxed: rows use dividers rather than repeated nested enclosures.

## Elevation & Depth

Paper surfaces are mostly flat, with fine boundaries and tonal course covers. Soft shadows lift the illustrated books; interaction adds temporary card depth, perspective tilt and icon bounce. The persistent Canvas constellation sits behind content without intercepting the pointer; the obsolete hero orbit is hidden. Flowing dashed source connections and rotating rounded rings make the Atlas hub active. Canvas energy is bounded to its hero and map surfaces.

- **Book at rest:** `0 9px 17px #00000026`.
- **Book CSS hover/focus:** `0 14px 25px #00000033`; active GSAP transforms can supersede the CSS transform.
- **Course pointer lift:** `0 24px 40px rgba(0,0,0,.16)` during the automatic fine-pointer lift.
- **Selected theme control:** `0 2px 5px #18204910`.
- **Atlas branch:** `0 6px 15px #00000013`; selected branches and the course hub use `0 8px 22px #0002` and `0 8px 20px #0002` respectively.
- **Original shared brand mark:** `-5px 5px 0 #e8f0ff`; this identity-specific offset shadow remains fixed across themes.

**The Quiet Reader Rule.** Keep discovery motion outside the reading content; use static constellation and stop spatial effects when system reduced motion is requested. Automatic discovery motion is the approved normal state; do not restore the rejected play button or header lightning toggle.

Discovery uses bundled GSAP for stronger spring hover, fine-pointer 3D tilt, magnetic buttons, draggable book links, particle feedback and source transitions. Cards lift by 17px and scale to 1.045. Dragging requires a fine pointer; movement beyond the drag threshold suppresses that click while ordinary clicks navigate. Motion ignores the obsolete `sem2_studio_motion=off` preference. Neither a hero play button nor a header lightning toggle exists.

The Canvas uses 56 constellation points, a capped pixel ratio of 1.5 and a drawing cadence of at least 30ms between frames. Pointer-down creates at most 68 radial burst particles plus a fading ring. Intersection and visibility observers pause offscreen/hidden Canvas and the CSS book/connection/ring loops, then resume them on return. Normal-state books float on a 5s loop, branch connections flow on a 2.2s loop, and hub rings turn in 21s and 34s opposing loops. These persistent loops are explicitly requested. Reduced motion leaves a static constellation and native controls, removes the spatial loops and suppresses bursts.

Study Rift adds a locally vendored Three.js 0.186.1 scene: a procedural torus knot transitions into 48 instanced tunnel rings with camera flight, 150 instanced fragments, pointer parallax and a bounded pointer-down impulse. Custom GLSL surface/background shaders and a render-target post pass provide iridescence, restrained bloom and chromatic dispersion. Midnight overlays and text shadows keep copy readable over the geometry; these are opening-scene materials, not new dashboard surface effects.

## Shapes

Rounded rectangular pages and shelf-like rows use the frontmatter radius vocabulary. Books carry softly rounded corners and small rotations; the original shared blue brand mark keeps its compact 20px by 26px silhouette, asymmetric 3px/3px/9px/3px corners, brand-blue fill and pale offset identity shadow on every viewport and theme. Its decorative `:after` is hidden; earlier Studio and phone mark variants do not win the final cascade. Thin strokes define fields and cards. SVG line icons carry actions and resource families, while colour dots identify courses. Atlas adds a 17px hub corner, 26px rotating-ring corner and 14px numbered-marker corner; its materials enclosure uses 16px corners. Phone branches and markers use 9px corners. These source-grounded additions expand the established radius vocabulary. Rift uses the existing 10px radius for its skip control and scroll-mouse outline; the tiny wheel indicator uses a 1px radius. Procedural knot/tunnel geometry is distinct from these control shapes.

## Components

### Buttons

Confident, compact controls: the primary button uses the theme accent and paper ink; the hero variant uses literal white with cobalt ink. The base button has a 45px minimum height, a 9px radius and 10px/18px padding. Hover darkens slightly; focus uses a visible 3px theme-focus outline with a 4px offset. Discovery buttons may respond magnetically when motion and fine-pointer support permit it.

### Chips

Unselected filters are transparent muted text. Selected filters use soft accent paper and stronger accent text at weight 600. They wrap in the desktop course tools, then spread across the available phone width. Atlas source-family buttons use a pale selected fill with dark cobalt ink; reading mode substitutes pale sage and forest ink. Hover raises branches by 5px and focus uses a warm 3px outline with a 4px offset. These are native buttons with pressed-state semantics, positioned around the hub on every viewport.

### Cards / Containers

Course cards have a thin line border, paper background and a 13px outer radius. A coloured 116px cover holds the subject icon, followed by a title, real resource counts and separated source actions; phone covers are 91px high. Cards have no resting shadow. The motion layer allows overflow for their lift and preserves rounded cover corners. Timetable rows do not inherit this enclosure.

### Inputs / Fields

Search fields use a paper surface, a line border, a 10px radius and a leading line icon. The library search nests a ground-colour field within its tools surface. Focus-within adds a 2px accent outline; native selects retain themed surfaces and readable options. Search matches Arabic/English catalog metadata, with subject/type selection and profile-aware TA visibility. Empty results expose a reset action.

### Navigation

The right rail uses icon/text routes with a soft accent current-page state, followed by colour-coded subjects. Phone bottom navigation preserves the same four destinations and current-page semantics. The compact header keeps the profile and theme cycle reachable; the rejected motion control is absent. A native subject select switches courses directly. Source choice remains native and retains the existing track, family and reading contracts.

### Book links and source shelves

Three working course links form an illustrated book stack. The final covers are mint Database Systems, pink Operating Systems and peach Data Structures. Mouse drag is a playful optional gesture, not a replacement for ordinary navigation. The books float automatically while their hero is visible; the rejected play control is absent.

Library rows pair a course-colour icon, a real resource title, course/type metadata and an open action. Continue rows and the home resume link derive only from local saved reader/quiz state; reader slide and tab are restored where available. An empty history invites an actual first study action instead of showing synthetic progress.

### Study Rift opening sequence

The four scroll phases are sculpture, transition, tunnel flight and arrival. Mouse movement changes camera perspective; pressing the scene applies a decaying impulse without affecting controls. A skip action and the scene wordmark return to the working home. This replaces the old processor and draggable material-node composition; neither is loaded. The rejected hero play button and header lightning toggle remain absent.

Home loads the lifecycle controller before profile-modal initialization, so onboarding waits for `portal:intro-end`. The loader reuses GSAP when present and loads only ScrollTrigger and Rift; Draggable and Inertia are not dependencies of this opening. Rift imports local pinned Three.js modules, with no runtime CDN. Native scroll scrubs a single GSAP driver with 0.55s smoothing. Hidden tabs pause rendering. Module or WebGL failure and live context loss switch to a scroll-responsive Canvas fallback; if the sequence cannot mount, timeout/failure restores the Studio. Cancellation during asynchronous imports does not leave an orphan scene. Exit disposes geometries, materials, instanced meshes, render target and renderer, cancels frame callbacks and releases the timeline, ScrollTrigger and observers.

**The Rift Entry Sequence Rule.** On eligible desktop home entry, run Study Rift once per session; forced eligible replay and its legacy query alias both open the new scene. Preserve native reverse scroll, a clear skip action, reduced-motion/phone bypass, graphics fallback and complete shell/GPU cleanup before normal Studio use. Home library, continue and timetable routes bypass it.

### Course atlas and numbered source lane

The real course icon forms the hub of five connected buttons: lectures, sections, video, assignments and references. Active selection brightens its connection, springs the core and transitions the corresponding source panel. The course selector and first-source action remain native controls; the action delegates to the first real lecture action. Staff and profile context sit in a disclosure rather than occupying a separate column. Section and assignment counts refresh when the student profile changes, with no fabricated values.

Numbered source rows preserve actual source order and all original open/study/download actions. Their ordinal markers are decorative, and explanation-capable rows use their course wash. Actions and detail disclosures occupy the content column, retaining a continuous full-width reading lane. On very narrow phones the marker disappears so the source and its actions keep the available width.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** preserve each course colour across navigation, cards, books and results.
- **Do** retain native course and resource controls, visible focus and all five source-map branches on phones.
- **Do** use actual catalog metadata and saved reader state for resource and continue views.
- **Do** provide abundant automatic discovery interaction, pause offscreen energy and preserve the system reduced-motion fallback.
- **Do** keep the primary course action ahead of the decorative book stack on phones.

### Don't:
- **Don't** revive the redundant track strip beside resource-family controls.
- **Don't** add decorative timetable eyebrows or nest extra bordered cards around its rows.
- **Don't** replace local display typography with a system display face.
- **Don't** fabricate progress, counts or learning completion to fill an empty view.
- **Don't** restore the rejected hero play button, header lightning toggle or obsolete saved-off behavior.
- **Don't** leave ordinary Studio scrolling or navigation blocked after Study Rift, replace the cursor or animate the reading content.
